---
title: "On plain text"
date: "2026-08-14"
---

Plain text outlives the tools that read it. That's the entire argument, but it's
worth spelling out.

A file of Markdown written in 2005 opens today in any editor on any operating
system, unchanged. A document written the same year in a proprietary format may
need a converter, a virtual machine, or a subscription — assuming the format is
documented at all.

The tradeoff is real: you give up rich layout, comments, and change tracking. For
notes, drafts, and anything you expect to reread in a decade, that's a good deal.
